Let tq(n) and pq(n) be the raw total and primitive counts of ordered integer
quadruples with sum n and sum of squares n^2. Signs and order are counted.
T(n)=tq(n)/4=A354766(n), P(n)=pq(n)/4=A278085(n).
The alternative primary definition of A278085, using primitive quadruples
of sum3n and norm3n^2, is equivalent by q -> (n,n,n,n)-q, preserving content.

For odd n,
P(n)=Product_{p^k||n} p^(k-1)*(p-chi(p)),
where chi(3)=0, chi(p)=1 for p==1 mod6, chi(p)=-1 for p==5 mod6.
The empty product is1. P(2*n)=P(n) for odd n; P(4*n)=0 for n>=1.
P is multiplicative.

T(n)=Sum_{d|n} P(d), and T is multiplicative.
For prime p and k>=1:
- T(2^k)=2;
- T(3^k)=(3^(k+1)-1)/2;
- p==1 mod6: T(p^k)=p^k;
- p==5 mod6: T(p^k)=p^k+2*(p^k-1)/(p-1).

T(n)=n iff n=2^e*m, where e=0 or1, m is odd, and every prime divisor of m
is1 mod6; m=1 is allowed. The set of these positive indices has natural
density zero. Thus the OEIS positive-density conjecture is false.

The corrected primitive bridge uses the primitive three-square count at
norm3*n^2 for odd n and norm3*(n/2)^2 for n==2 mod4; it is zero for4|n.
The count is proved by concrete prime-neighbor lattices, including p=3.

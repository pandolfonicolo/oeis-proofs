Primary entry: A242322.

Let r=k+2 and E_r={(i,j): 1<=i<j<=r}.  State (i,j) records the two
missing positive last-occurrence ages among 0,...,r.

Transitions:
- if j<r, (i,j) -> (i+1,j+1);
- if j=r, let v=i+1; successors are every pair incident with v.

Initial state weight:
    w(i,j)=i*(j-1).

Exact count and generating function:
    T(n,k)=w^T A_k^(n-1) 1,
    sum_{n>=1} T(n,k)x^n = x w^T (I-x A_k)^(-1) 1.

The matrix has N=binomial(k+2,2) states and determinant +/-1.  If
det(I-xA_k)=sum_{ell=0}^N d_ell x^ell, then
    sum_{ell=0}^N d_ell T(n-ell,k)=0
for n>=N+1.

The scalar order N is minimal for every k>=1, even if the recurrence is
required only eventually. A boundary reflection argument proves cyclicity;
a unimodular reversal form gives observability, and every shifted Hankel
matrix is invertible. No conjectured determinant evaluation is assumed.

Stable rows, for k>=max(1,n-4):
    T(n,k)=a_n*k^4/8+b_n*k^3/12+c_n*k^2/8+(12*n+19)*k/12+1,
where
    a_n=4*3^(n-1)-2^(n+1)+1,
    b_n=-4*3^n+(6*n+9)*2^n-6*n-1,
    c_n=4*3^(n-1)+(12-4*n)*2^n+4*n^2-5.

The boundary is uniformly sharp: T(6,1)=149, whereas the polynomial gives

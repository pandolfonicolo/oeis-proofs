# Revision notes

The revised manuscript was rebuilt around one central theorem rather than several loosely connected claims.

## Mathematical corrections

1. The quotient automaton is identified explicitly as a bouquet of directed cycles of lengths `1,...,k+1`.
2. The previously omitted general cancellation is proved algebraically.
3. The exact numerator is
   \[
   N_k(x)=\sum_{j=1}^{k+1}\frac{(k+2-j)(kj+2)}2x^j.
   \]
4. The incorrect numerator rows for `k=5,6,8` were replaced by values generated from the formula.
5. The empirical stable-row formula on A242239 is now proved for `k>=n-3`.
6. The A145113 generating function is correctly described as already known, not as a new result.
7. Mathar's identity is stated as an OEIS sequence identity for `n>=2`; the `n=1` case is explicitly identified as a polynomial extension of A004767.
8. The scope is narrowed to covering windows whose length is exactly alphabet size plus one.

## Reproducibility corrections

- The package is organized as a self-contained project inside the `oeis-proofs` repository, with separate manuscript, verification, OEIS, and documentation directories.
- All displayed numerator values are generated by the supplied verifier.
- The verifier uses three independent counting models: the bouquet, a raw last-block DFA, and literal canonical-word enumeration.
- It checks current displayed A242239 data, the stable rows, A242235 through `n=210`, A145113, and the exact polynomial identity for Mathar's formula.
- The Makefile propagates verifier failures and fixes the PDF source date for byte-for-byte reproducible clean builds.
- The manuscript avoids the nonessential `enumitem` dependency and preserves the correct lemma, proposition, and corollary names in cross-references.
- The public PDF contains no browser-export headers, chat URLs, or unreferenced claims of certification.

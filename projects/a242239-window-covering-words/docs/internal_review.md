## Proof audit

The review checked the following dependencies in order:

1. The relabeling action is free because the first valid window contains every
   alphabet symbol, and each orbit therefore has one canonical representative.
2. Every length-`q` block has either `q` distinct symbols or exactly one doubled
   and one missing symbol.
3. The stated transition rules follow directly from the covering condition for
   the newly formed length-`q+1` window.
4. Defect-pair states decompose into directed cycles of lengths `2,...,q`,
   together with the rainbow self-loop, giving the bouquet
   `C_1 ∨ ... ∨ C_q`.
5. Every quotient state contains exactly `q!` concrete blocks, so canonical
   words correspond to unrestricted-start, unrestricted-end bouquet walks.
6. The walk decomposition into complete hub returns and a proper final cycle
   prefix gives the claimed denominator and auxiliary polynomial.
7. The cancellation using `(1-x)H_k(x)=D_k(x)+kx` is exact, and coefficient
   extraction gives the stated numerator.
8. The recurrence range follows from the degree and positivity of the
   numerator.
9. The stable-row boundary `k >= n-3` and the first excluded defect are
   consistent with direct computation.
10. The specialization `k=4` and the rational-function identity with A145113
    imply Mathar's formula with the stated indexing caveat at `n=1`.
